Support

Get in touch

Email support@auradating.app and we will come back to you within two working days.

If it is urgent — a safety concern, or something that happened on a date — say so in the subject line and we will prioritise it.

Deleting your account

Open the app, go to Profile → Settings → Privacy, and choose Delete my account. This removes your profile, your photos, your messages and your date history. It cannot be undone.

If you cannot get into the app, email us from the address you signed up with and we will delete it for you.

Subscriptions and tokens

Aura Gold renews through your Apple ID. To cancel, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone — cancelling inside Aura is not possible, because Apple owns the billing.

Tokens buy your place in an evening. If something goes wrong and a token is spent on a date that never happened, email us and we will put it back.

Safety

Every member is ID-verified before they can be matched. You can block or report anyone from their profile or from a date, and reports are read by a person, not a filter.

If you ever feel unsafe on a date, the app has an SOS screen with your emergency contacts and the venue address ready to share.

Trouble signing in

Aura supports email, Apple and Google sign-in. If you signed up with Apple and hid your email, use the same Apple ID — a different one creates a separate account.
